## Workflow

1. Extract the current brand read: offer, tone, visual language, trust level, asset style and motion character.
2. Identify protected content: routes, nav labels, form fields, legal, SEO, analytics-sensitive text and real proof.
3. Inspect brand layers: promise, voice, color, typography, layout rhythm, surface language, icons, product assets and motion.
4. Assign each layer one decision: Preserve, Evolve, Remove, Introduce or Protect.
5. Cite evidence for every decision: screenshot path, source file, CSS token, asset path, copy snippet, route, nav, form or SEO constraint.
6. Write preserve, evolve, remove and introduce lists.
7. Push the decisions into the style tile, storyboard, asset queue, task graph and handoff.

## Blocking Conditions

Block or mark high risk if:

- CTA text, route, form field or legal/SEO copy is changed without approval;
- real proof is replaced with decorative or fake visual material;
- current brand colors or assets are discarded without evidence;
- a reference style overwrites the project identity;
- the style tile introduces colors, fonts, radii, shadows, icons or asset styles that contradict the brand DNA map.

## Decision Words

Use only:

- Preserve
- Evolve
- Remove
- Introduce
- Protect

Do not use vague decisions like "modernize" or "make premium".
