# Landing Copy Offer Audit

Use this skill before visual polish. A beautiful landing page with unclear offer, weak CTA, fake proof, or vague claims will still fail.

## Inputs

- Existing landing copy, screenshot, URL, or `landing-source-dossier.md`.
- Product, audience, offer, and conversion goal if known.
- Traffic source if known.
- Proof points, testimonials, metrics, guarantees, demos, or pricing if available.

## Workflow

1. Extract the current promise.
   - What is sold?
   - Who is it for?
   - What outcome is promised?
   - What action should the visitor take?

2. Identify the single primary CTA.
   - Keep one CTA intent across nav, hero, and final CTA.
   - Rewrite weak labels like "Learn more" into specific action labels.
   - Do not invent a CTA the product cannot fulfill.

3. Check the offer.
   - What does the user get?
   - How fast or easy is it?
   - What risk is removed?
   - What proof makes the claim believable?
   - Read `план разработки топового лендинга/54-proof-integrity-source-map.md` and assign claim verdicts for visible claims and proof.

4. Map objections.
   - Cost or time.
   - Trust and credibility.
   - Switching effort.
   - Security/privacy.
   - "Will this work for someone like me?"

5. Rewrite section jobs.
   - Hero: outcome and CTA.
   - Proof: credibility near the claim.
   - Problem/solution: why now.
   - Benefits: specific outcomes, not feature dump.
   - How it works: 3 clear actions, not generic "Step 1".
   - FAQ: answer real objections.
   - Final CTA: same intent as hero.

## Copy Rules

- No fake numbers.
- No fake logos.
- No fake testimonials.
- No vague verbs: "elevate", "unleash", "revolutionize", "seamless" unless the brand literally uses them.
- No duplicate CTA intent.
- No long hero subhead. Target 20 words or less.
- Use the user's real product vocabulary.

## Quality Gate

Before handing off, confirm:

- One offer.
- One audience.
- One primary action.
- Claim/proof pairs are explicit.
- Unsupported proof is verified, rephrased, backlogged or rejected.
- Objections are handled before final CTA.
- Every visible string was reread for clarity.
