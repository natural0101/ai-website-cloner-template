## Workflow

1. Extract product category, buyer, CTA, buyer anxiety, workflow, asset type, desired emotion and important sections.
2. Choose sources from `52-reference-gallery-source-map.md` before searching, based on direct/domain, adjacent/theme, workflow/flow, visual language, motion/component, evidence and anti-reference lanes.
3. Search across direct/domain, adjacent/theme, workflow/flow, visual language, motion/component, analogous and anti-reference lanes.
4. For every candidate, write a closeness reason. Reject candidates that are only visually interesting.
5. Record query, source, URL, access status, lane, section, what it may inform, what not to copy and risk.
6. Build a closeness matrix across product, audience, CTA, trust model, workflow, asset type, visual emotion and interaction.
7. Promote only references that have a concrete section use and acceptable risk.
8. Use anti-references to create do-not-do rules.

## Minimum Mix

Collect at least:

- 2 direct/domain candidates;
- 2 adjacent/theme candidates;
- 1 workflow/flow candidate when signup, onboarding, checkout, booking, dashboard or product UI matters;
- 1 visual language candidate;
- 1 motion/component candidate when animation or special components are planned;
- 1 anti-reference in crowded or generic markets.

## Rejection Rules

Reject if:

- no live URL or screenshot evidence exists;
- source access status is unknown or unrecorded;
- no closeness reason can be named;
- it would force fake proof, fake screenshots or copied claims;
- it pushes the page toward generic AI/SaaS sameness;
- it depends on inaccessible premium/pro source;
- motion creates accessibility, mobile or performance risk;
- it contradicts brand, CTA or buyer trust model.
